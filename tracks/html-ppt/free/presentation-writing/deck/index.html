06 · the split and the payoff · 1 of 2Medici

The mechanism, part one

One position becomes two claims that always add back to it.

Deposit & strike

Deposit a tokenized asset, pick a strike; the vault issues P (capped, income-bearing) and N (everything above the strike).

The identity

P + N ≡ collateral at every price — closed-form, verified as math rather than asserted at runtime.

The oracle

Read once, at settlement. There is no live feed to manipulate during the position's life.

collateral →   P  +  N   → recombine or settle, atomically

08 · the cost, and what this instrument isMedici

The cost

P is short a call — that decline is what P earns.

  • Above the strike P declines continuously as S/x: no floor, no cliff, computable at every price — and that decline is the income.
  • Roll execution is the dominant cost: −1.2%/yr at 10bps across ~12.2 rolls a year; best sub-period gross theta was +0.57%/yr in 2021–22.

−1.25% /yr

Net drift; P ends at $0.895 on $1.00 over a 9-year backtest — Hypercall, Aug 2017 – Jun 2026; ETH underlying, Deribit options, Black-Scholes at mid.
