PL. 06DIAGRAMSERIES OF 14

One position becomes two claims that always add back to it

THE OPERATION
deposit a tokenized asset, pick a strike; the vault issues P (capped, income-bearing) and N (everything above the strike)
THE IDENTITY
P + N is identically the collateral at every price — closed-form, verified as math rather than asserted at runtime
THE ORACLE
read once, at settlement; there is no live feed to manipulate during the position's life

PL. 07DIAGRAMSERIES OF 14

At maturity there are two cases, and neither is a margin call

AT OR BELOW THE STRIKE
S ≥ x: P receives 1.00, the whole collateral; N receives 0.00
ABOVE THE STRIKE
S < x: P receives S/x; N receives 1 − S/x
THEN DRAW THE THRESHOLD
there is no reference price at which the vault is worth less than the collateral, so it has to be taken away
