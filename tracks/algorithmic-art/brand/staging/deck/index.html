03

Change

Money markets assume collateral you can sell today; RWA collateral isn't.

  • What a money market assumes: market pricing, continuous liquidity, sale in one transaction.
  • What tokenized RWA collateral is: NAV pricing, monthly or quarterly redemption windows, “cannot liquidate its position in a single onchain transaction”.
  • Consequence: the liquidation pathway has to be built by hand, per issuer.

06

Pivot

The fix is not better discipline; it is removing liquidation entirely.

  • Every mitigation since is procedural: curators, LTV policy, de-leverage frameworks, signed agreements.
  • The mechanism underneath is unchanged: a threshold exists, and thresholds get breached.
  • Removing the threshold removes the whole category of failure.

07

The mechanism

Split the collateral into P and N; they always sum back.

  • At maturity, one reference price x is read against strike S.
  • S ≥ x: P receives 1.00, N receives 0.00.
  • S < x: P receives S/x, N receives 1 − S/x.
  • P + N ≡ collateral at every price; no under-collateralization condition exists in the payoff function.
  • Nothing is borrowed, so nothing can be called.

08

Move

Same collateral, two venues: one can force-sell it, one cannot.

  • Trigger for a forced sale: liquidation threshold breach vs none in the payoff function.
  • Price feed required: continuous, manipulable mid-position vs one read at settlement.
  • Off-chain machinery required: issuer liquidation agreements and de-leverage frameworks vs none.
  • Where the incumbent wins, stated: $620M+ of live RWA deposits, years of operating history, an existing borrower base. Medici has no live users.

The cost

The cost is real: P gives up everything above the strike.

  • P is economically short a call above the strike; above it, value declines as P = S/x, with no floor.
  • The decline is continuous and computable, never a cliff and never a margin call — and it is the source of P's income.
  • 9-year backtest (Hypercall, Aug 2017–Jun 2026, ETH, Deribit): net drift −1.25%/yr, P ends at $0.895 on $1.00.
  • Roll cost −1.2%/yr at 10bps across ~12.2 rolls/yr is the dominant cost driver.
  • The backtest is third-party and ETH-only; an RWA/MMF underlying has different volatility characteristics.
