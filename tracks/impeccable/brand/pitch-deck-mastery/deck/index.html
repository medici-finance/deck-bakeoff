Notice 03 / 14The solution

One position becomes two claims that always sum back to it

3.1

P, the Protected leg — full collateral value at or below strike, no margin call, no forced sale. N, the Amplified leg — everything above strike.

3.2

P + N is identically the collateral, at every price, by construction. Split, hold, then recombine or settle at maturity — atomically.

Notice 04 / 14The payoff

At maturity, P takes S/x and N takes the rest

P — protected leg, S/x above strikeN — amplified leg, 1 − S/x above strike
x ≤ S  P receives 1.00, N receives 0.00
x > S  P receives S/x, N receives 1 − S/x
4.1

Every row sums to exactly the collateral. Above strike, P declines continuously — a slope, never a cliff. The oracle is read once, at settlement, not continuously.

Notice 06 / 14Who holds each legRedStone, as of March 2026

Two legs, two holders, each with an intrinsic reason

P — the Protected leg
N — the Amplified leg
Fund treasury or issuer takes P — income on an asset they keep, never sold, never called.
Allocator or speculator takes N — amplified exposure, no liquidation price, no funding rate.
6.1

Volatile collateral plus two intrinsic holders equals endogenous two-sided demand; selling P is the liquidity, with no repayment schedule.

6.2

The split needs volatility to price N: treasuries have least of it, gold and equity-linked most.
