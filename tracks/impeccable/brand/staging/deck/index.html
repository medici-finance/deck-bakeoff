Notice 08 / 14The mechanism

Split the collateral into P and N; they always sum back.

P — protected leg, S/x above strikeN — amplified leg, 1 − S/x above strike
S ≥ x  P receives 1.00, N receives 0.00
S < x  P receives S/x, N receives 1 − S/x
8.1

At maturity, one reference price x is read against strike S. P + N ≡ collateral at every price; no under-collateralization condition exists in the payoff function — and nothing is borrowed, so nothing can be called.
