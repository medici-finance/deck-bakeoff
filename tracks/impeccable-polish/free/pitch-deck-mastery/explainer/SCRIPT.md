# Explainer script — Medici for RWA tokens (pitch-deck-mastery cut)

**Contract:** `messaging/pitch-deck-mastery/staging.yaml`, video lane · 8 scenes ·
**143 s total** · 332 words · 141 wpm. Scene durations equal the contract's `vo_seconds` exactly
(the 2 s hold inside scene 5 is inside its 21 s).
**Player:** `explainer.html` — 1920×1080, auto-plays on load. `?scene=N` starts at a scene,
`?at=<sec>` freezes a scene at a given second (headless capture), Space/→ next, ← previous,
P pauses. `window.__player = {scene, t, total, seek(n)}` (plus `freeze(t)`).
**No audio, no TTS.** VO below is read live or dubbed at render time.

**The kinetic argument:** one liquidation gesture, performed twice. Scene 2 completes it and takes
the collateral. Scene 5 begins it from identical markup and identical keyframes, and stops.
