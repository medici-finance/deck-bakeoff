PROBLEM

RWA lending's hardest problem is the liquidation nobody can execute.

  • Every venue prices your collateral against a liquidation threshold.
  • Tokenized funds cannot be sold in one transaction.
  • So the workout is hand-built: signed liquidation agreements with issuers, formal de-leverage frameworks, curators with a line to the issuer.

the splitMedici

SOLUTION

One position becomes two claims that always sum back to it.

P — the Protected leg

Full collateral value at or below strike. No margin call, no forced sale.

N — the Amplified leg

Everything above the strike.

The identity

P + N ≡ collateral, at every price, by construction.

split  →  hold  →  recombine or settle at maturity  —  atomically

03
the split · the bold momentMedici

PROOF

No liquidation threshold exists because no under-collateralization condition exists.

  • "No forced liquidation" is a theorem, not a risk parameter.
  • Closed-form invariant, verified as pure math — not a runtime assert.
  • There is no price x at which the vault is short of collateral.
  • Nothing to breach, so nothing to seize, so no liquidation agreement to sign.

04
who holds each legMedici

APPLICATION

Two legs, two holders, each with an intrinsic reason.

Fund treasury or issuer takes P

Income on an asset they keep — never sold, never called.

Allocator or speculator takes N

Amplified exposure, no liquidation price, no funding rate.

  • Volatile collateral plus two intrinsic holders equals endogenous two-sided demand.
  • Selling P is the liquidity, with no repayment schedule.
  • The split needs volatility to price N: treasuries have least of it, gold and equity-linked most.

risk and costMedici

RISK

Every risk we removed, and every risk we did not.

Removed

Forced liquidation, structurally. Counterparty risk to Medici — the vault holds your deposit.

Present by design

Principal risk above strike: P is short a call, P = S/x, no floor. Net drift −1.25%/yr over the backtest; P ends at $0.895 on $1.00.

Present & open

Smart-contract risk — not yet audited; two independent DAML audits are Phase 1. N liquidity. Regulatory classification, counsel review not yet done.

Unchanged: the collateral's own custody chain sits upstream of the vault.
