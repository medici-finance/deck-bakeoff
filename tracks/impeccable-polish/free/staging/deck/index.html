04 / 14 · change · comparisonMedici

The assumption underneath

Money markets assume collateral you can sell today; RWA collateral isn't.

What a money market assumes

  • Market pricing
  • Continuous liquidity
  • Sale in one transaction

What tokenized RWA collateral is

  • NAV pricing
  • Monthly or quarterly redemption windows
  • "Cannot liquidate its position in a single onchain transaction"

Consequence: the liquidation pathway has to be built by hand, per issuer.

07 / 14 · consequence · pivotMedici

The turn

The fix is not better discipline; it is removing liquidation entirely.

  • Every mitigation since is procedural: curators, LTV policy, de-leverage frameworks, signed agreements.
  • The mechanism underneath is unchanged: a threshold exists, and thresholds get breached.
  • Removing the threshold removes the whole category of failure.

08 / 14 · move · mechanismMedici

The one mechanism

Split the collateral into P and N; they always sum back.

At maturity, one reference price x is read against strike S.

CaseP receivesN receives
S ≥ x1.000.00
S < xS / x1 − S / x

P + N ≡ collateral at every price; no under-collateralization condition exists in the payoff function. Nothing is borrowed, so nothing can be called.

11 / 14 · standing · what it costsMedici

The cost, volunteered

The cost is real: P gives up everything above the strike.

  • P is economically short a call above the strike; above it, value declines as P = S/x, with no floor.
  • The decline is continuous and computable — never a cliff and never a margin call — and it is the source of P's income.
  • Roll cost −1.2%/yr at 10bps across ~12.2 rolls/yr is the dominant cost driver.

9-year backtest · net drift

−1.25% /yr

Hypercall, Aug 2017 – Jun 2026, ETH underlying, Deribit. P ends at $0.895 on $1.00.

The backtest is third-party and ETH-only; an RWA/MMF underlying has different volatility characteristics.
