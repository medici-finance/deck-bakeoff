**Total runtime: 120 s** (9 scenes). Durations are the contract's `vo_seconds`, unchanged.

**Controls.** Auto-plays on load. `?scene=N` starts at scene N; `→`/`←` step scenes; `Space` pauses; `R` restarts; `C` hides the scene counter (or load with `?chrome=0`) for a clean capture. `window.__player = {scene, t, total, seek(n)}` is exposed for tooling.

**Motion contract.** Every scene's motion is its `motion_role` from the staging file, not a chosen effect: hold / accumulate / strain / rupture / hold / assemble / hold / divest / cadence. Peaks are s4 (rupture, energy 5) and s6 (assemble, energy 4); the quiet scenes s1, s5 and s8 move only on entrance, which is what pays for them.
