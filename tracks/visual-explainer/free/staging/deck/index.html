  • At maturity, one reference price x is read against strike S.

objection

The cost is real: P gives up everything above the strike.

  • P is economically short a call above the strike; above it, value declines as P = S/x, with no floor.
  • The decline is continuous and computable, never a cliff and never a margin call — and it is the source of P’s income.
  • 9-year backtest (Hypercall, Aug 2017–Jun 2026, ETH, Deribit): net drift −1.25%/yr, P ends at $0.895 on $1.00.
  • Roll cost −1.2%/yr at 10bps across ~12.2 rolls/yr is the dominant cost driver.
  • The backtest is third-party and ETH-only; an RWA/MMF underlying has different volatility characteristics.
objection
