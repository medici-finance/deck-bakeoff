The mismatch

Money markets assume collateral you can sell today; RWA collateral isn't.

What a money market assumes

  • Market pricing
  • Continuous liquidity
  • Sale in one transaction

What tokenized RWA collateral is

  • NAV pricing
  • Monthly or quarterly redemption windows
  • “cannot liquidate its position in a single onchain transaction”

Consequence: the liquidation pathway has to be built by hand, per issuer.

The mechanism

Split the collateral into P and N; they always sum back.

At maturity, one reference price x is read against strike S.

S ≥ x P receives 1.00 N receives 0.00
S < x P receives S/x N receives 1 − S/x

P + N ≡ collateral at every price. No under-collateralization condition exists in the payoff function.

Nothing is borrowed, so nothing can be called.

The cost, named

The cost is real: P gives up everything above the strike.

Above S, P declines as S/x — continuous, computable, no floor.
