The pivot

Medici deletes the liquidation pathway instead of managing it

  • Split the collateral into two claims that always add back to it; then there is no threshold to cross.
  • No under-collateralization condition exists anywhere in the payoff function.
Medici Canton briefing slide 5; Medici asset-manager briefing slide 4 05

The mechanism, part one

One position becomes two claims that always add back to it

  • Deposit a tokenized asset, pick a strike; the vault issues P (capped, income-bearing) and N (everything above the strike).
  • P + N is identically the collateral at every price — closed-form, verified as math rather than asserted at runtime.
  • The oracle is read once, at settlement; there is no live feed to manipulate during the position's life.
Medici Canton briefing slides 3 · 5; Medici asset-manager briefing slide 3 06

The mechanism, part two

At maturity there are two cases, and neither is a margin call

S ≥ x P receives 1.00 N receives 0.00
S < x P receives S/x N receives 1 − S/x

Then the liquidation threshold every other venue needs is drawn — and there is no reference price at which the vault is worth less than the collateral, so it has to be taken away.
